The admin features for LankaServe include the following functionalities:
	1.	Comprehensive Admin Dashboard
	•	Central panel to track all system activities.
	•	Real-time views of user data, service requests, performance metrics, and notifications.
	•	Efficient management and supervision of the platform.
	2.	User Management
	•	Approve, deactivate, or manage users and service providers.
	•	Verify provider credentials and enforce platform policies.
	•	Protect system integrity and prevent misuse.
	3.	Job and Advertisement Management
	•	Review or remove irrelevant job postings.
	•	Create, schedule, and manage promotional banners or advertisements.
	•	Support content moderation for a professional marketplace.
	4.	Analytics and Reporting System
	•	Real-time data analysis with charts and reports.
	•	Identify most demanded services, geographic patterns, and customer satisfaction metrics.
	•	Automated summaries to aid decision-making.
	5.	Feedback Monitoring and Review Management
	•	Review customer feedback with predefined tags and keywords.
	•	Identify service issues and resolve them efficiently.
	•	Support continuous improvement of service quality.
	6.	Automated Notification Control
	•	Send announcements, promotions, or important updates to users.
	•	Ensure consistent communication across the platform.
	7.	Data Backup and System Monitoring
	•	Regular backups to prevent data loss or corruption.
	•	Continuous monitoring to ensure uptime, reliability, and security compliance.
	8.	Service Verification Monitoring
	•	Track QR scan timestamps, arrival confirmations, and job completion approvals.
	•	Audit disputes related to service delivery authenticity.

These features allow administrators to maintain trust, manage users and content, monitor service performance, and ensure platform reliability.
